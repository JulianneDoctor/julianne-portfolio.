What the form saves

When you submit an inquiry, the website saves your name, email address, message, submission date, and consent record so Julianne can respond and manage the conversation. Please do not include passwords, financial information, or other sensitive records.

Where it goes

When the database is connected, messages are stored in Julianne’s Supabase project. Access to inquiries through this application requires an approved administrator account and two-step verification. The project owner also has access through the database provider.

Cloudflare Turnstile verifies contact submissions to help block bots. The hosting provider, Supabase, and Cloudflare may process connection information, including IP addresses, as part of operating their services. The application itself does not add your IP address to your inquiry record. A secret-keyed digest of your email is used for submission limits; old limit records are removed when later submissions are processed.

Keeping and deleting messages

Julianne can delete inquiries through her dashboard when they are no longer needed. This version does not automatically delete inquiries after a set period. Provider logs and backups may retain records according to the provider’s settings and retention periods.

To request deletion, use the contact email listed on the portfolio contact section or send a request identifying your earlier inquiry.

Browser storage

The website does not add analytics or advertising trackers. Admin login tokens stay in browser memory rather than local storage. Turnstile may use browser storage or cookies as described in Cloudflare’s privacy information.
